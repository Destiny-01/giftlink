# Nomine Brand Foundation

## Brand decision

**Nomine** is the parent brand for the Claim Layer and its economic inbox.

Pronunciation: **NOH-mi-neh**.

The name draws from the idea of value established *in someone’s name*. It reflects the product’s defining capability: funded value can be addressed to a person, group, condition, or machine before the final receiving wallet or settlement route is known.

**Master line:** **Value, addressed.**

Nomine must be presented as a serious financial and protocol brand. It is broader than gifting, rewards, airdrops, bounties, grants, payroll, payments, or any single blockchain.

## Brand architecture

- **Nomine** — the parent brand and network.
- **Nomine Inbox** — the claimant-facing economic inbox where people discover, understand, settle, and track funded Claims.
- **Nomine Studio** — the issuer-facing environment for creating, funding, publishing, monitoring, and reconciling Claim programs.
- **Nomine Protocol** — the contracts, APIs, SDKs, Resolver interfaces, and settlement infrastructure beneath Nomine experiences and third-party integrations.
- **GiftLink by Nomine** — the original, focused gifting application: sending funded value through a link before the sender knows the recipient’s wallet.

Future verticals may join the Nomine family when they become real products. They should not be named or presented as existing products prematurely.

**Claim** remains the core product object. **Economic inbox** remains the claimant-facing product concept. These terms should not be replaced by the company name.

## Brand meaning

Wallet addresses tell money where to go immediately. Nomine makes value addressable before its final destination is known. An issuer can fund value, name who or what it is for, define the rules governing access and settlement, and establish what happens if it remains unused.

Nomine therefore stands for:

- Value backed by real, visible capital.
- A destination expressed through identity, eligibility, intent, or conditions rather than only a wallet address.
- Rules that remain clear from funding through settlement or recovery.
- A human destination for discovering value already addressed to the claimant.
- Infrastructure that protocols and applications can integrate without reducing Nomine to one use case.

## Core language

| Context | Language |
|---|---|
| Master brand | **Value, addressed.** |
| Consumer | **What’s yours should find you.** |
| Issuer | **Fund value now. Define how it can be claimed.** |
| Developer | **Make value claimable.** |
| Protocol | **Capital, conditions, and settlement in one programmable object.** |
| GiftLink | **Send value before you know the wallet.** |

The preferred vocabulary is: **fund, address, qualify, claim, settle, route, recover, verify**.

Nomine should not describe itself with token hype, exaggerated decentralization claims, or language associated mainly with promotions and airdrops. Product communication should state who funded a Claim, what value backs it, who qualifies, which party verifies eligibility, what settlement does, when it expires, and where unused capital goes.

Examples of the intended voice:

- “This Claim is funded.”
- “Available until 24 September.”
- “Eligibility is verified by DevRel Collective.”
- “Settle 250 USDC to your wallet.”
- “Unused capital returns to the issuer.”

## Visual direction

Nomine should feel like serious financial infrastructure with a human front door: precise, calm, restrained, and recognizable.

### Palette

- **Onyx:** `#0B0B0D`
- **Warm ivory:** `#F3F0E9`
- **Graphite:** `#6B6B72`
- **Signal violet:** `#665CF6`
- **Limestone:** `#E5E1D8` — secondary light surfaces.
- **Deep violet:** `#5546D9` — accessible actions and accent text on ivory.
- **Pale violet:** `#B7AEFF` — restrained accent text and focus on dark surfaces.

The system is predominantly black and warm white. Signal violet is used deliberately for action, availability, and verified state rather than as decoration. Arc may appear as an ecosystem association, but Nomine should not imitate Arc’s identity.

### Typography

- **Instrument Sans** for brand and interface typography.
- **IBM Plex Mono** for amounts, wallet addresses, transaction references, receipts, and protocol data.

### Symbol direction

The approved mark is the **original Claim Register symbol from round 01, route 03**: rectangular register blocks in the exact geometry preserved in `brand/approved/logos/nomine-symbol-ink.svg`. The earlier endpoint/path exploration is superseded. The sliced diagonal N introduced in round 02 was explicitly rejected by the user.

Use the canonical SVGs unchanged. Do not redraw an N, interpret the mark from prose, or substitute exploration assets. Outlined wordmark lockups, inverse marks, avatars and a favicon are supplied in `brand/approved/logos/`.

Avoid gift boxes, coins, chain-link symbols, wallet hexagons, envelopes, rainbow palettes, excessive gradients, and generic Web3 imagery.

### Interface character

- Strong typography and generous whitespace.
- Thin structural borders and disciplined grids.
- Colour reserved for meaningful states.
- Funding proof, conditions, expiry, trust source, settlement status, and receipts treated as first-class information.
- Clear financial language instead of celebratory promotional styling.

## Relationship to GiftLink

GiftLink is product evidence and a focused application within Nomine. It is not the name or boundary of the broader system.

The public relationship is **GiftLink by Nomine**. GiftLink may retain a warmer, more personal expression appropriate to gifting, while inheriting Nomine’s trust, typography, state language, and infrastructure endorsement.

The distinction should remain easy to explain:

- **Nomine** makes funded value addressable and claimable.
- **GiftLink** uses Nomine to let someone send a gift before knowing the recipient’s wallet.

## Brand guardrails

- Do not position Nomine primarily as a gifting app, reward platform, airdrop tool, campaign manager, escrow service, wallet, or payment processor.
- Do not let the first Arc campaign define the overall category.
- Do not present speculative verticals as shipped products.
- Do not invent a protocol token as part of the brand.
- Do not imply that off-chain eligibility facts are trustless; name the responsible Resolver or issuer.
- Do not allow issuer marketing to turn Nomine Inbox into an advertising feed.
- Keep Arc and USDC prominent where they are technically relevant without making either the master brand identity.
