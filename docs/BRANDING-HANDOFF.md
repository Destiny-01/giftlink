# Branding Workstream Handoff

Use the following prompt to start the dedicated Nomine branding task:

> We are ready to develop the complete brand identity for **Nomine**. You own this branding workstream end to end and should make the work concrete through written guidelines and reviewable visual assets.
>
> Begin by reading `docs/BRAND.md`, `docs/PRODUCT.md`, and `docs/DECISIONS.md`. Treat those three documents as authoritative. Read `docs/product-architecture.md` only when you need more product context. Inspect the existing GiftLink frontend and current visual assets so you understand what exists, but do not mistake the legacy MVP for approved brand or production architecture.
>
> The approved decisions are:
>
> - **Nomine** is the parent brand for the Claim Layer and economic inbox.
> - Pronunciation: **NOH-mi-neh**.
> - Master line: **Value, addressed.**
> - The principal surfaces are **Nomine Inbox**, **Nomine Studio**, and **Nomine Protocol**.
> - The original application becomes **GiftLink by Nomine**.
> - A **Claim** remains the core funded product object; “economic inbox” remains the claimant-facing concept.
> - Nomine must represent infrastructure and a user-facing network, not merely gifting, rewards, airdrops, campaigns, escrow, or payments.
> - The overall aesthetic is predominantly black and warm white with one restrained accent. Avoid rainbow styling, generic crypto imagery, gift boxes, coins, chain links, wallet hexagons, excessive gradients, and superficial futuristic effects.
>
> First, perform a concise brand audit and a current collision check for the name, relevant domains, and major social handles. This is preliminary screening rather than legal advice; do not reopen the approved name unless you discover a material conflict. Then build a complete, coherent identity system for Nomine.
>
> Cover the following:
>
> 1. Brand strategy: purpose, category, promise, principles, audiences, positioning, differentiation, personality, and the central narrative connecting funded Claims with the economic inbox.
> 2. Brand architecture: rules for Nomine, Nomine Inbox, Nomine Studio, Nomine Protocol, GiftLink by Nomine, partner integrations, and future verticals without inventing products that do not exist.
> 3. Verbal identity: voice, tone by audience, vocabulary, prohibited language, one-line descriptions, short and long descriptions, headlines, calls to action, proof language, boilerplate, social bio, founder introduction, and messaging examples for claimants, issuers, developers, and ecosystem partners.
> 4. Visual identity: wordmark, symbol, lockups, monochrome behavior, safe space, minimum size, colour system, typography, grid, spacing, shape language, iconography, imagery, data and receipt presentation, motion principles, and accessibility requirements.
> 5. Product application: demonstrate how the identity behaves across a landing page, Nomine Inbox, Nomine Studio, developer documentation, a Claim page, transaction receipt, social avatar/banner, pitch deck cover, and GiftLink endorsement.
> 6. Arc relationship: show how “built on Arc” and USDC support can be communicated clearly without allowing Arc or Circle branding to dominate Nomine.
> 7. Brand governance: practical rules that keep future product, marketing, and partner work consistent.
>
> Develop two or three genuinely distinct visual routes rooted in the approved strategy, recommend the strongest one, and explain the tradeoffs plainly. The routes must feel like plausible identities for durable financial infrastructure, not moodboard variations or generic Web3 concepts. Give me reviewable visuals before treating the logo as final.
>
> Use SVG or other editable vector-native formats for identity marks and diagrams. Test the preferred mark at small sizes, in one colour, on light and dark backgrounds, and without the wordmark. Use raster generation only for supporting imagery where appropriate, not as the source of truth for the logo.
>
> Create persistent brand guidelines and organized editable assets in the repository. Keep exploratory work clearly separate from approved assets. Do not modify the production application or replace its current branding until I explicitly approve a visual route. Work iteratively with me, but make strong recommendations and carry out the production work rather than only describing what could be done.
>
> Start by confirming your understanding of the product and brand foundation in a concise way, then present the brand audit and the first set of visual routes.
