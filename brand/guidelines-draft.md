# Nomine identity guidelines — draft 0.2

Claim Register was selected by the user on 21 September 2026. Round-01 alternatives remain historical explorations. Round-02 mark and palette refinements await review. Approved foundations are inherited from `docs/BRAND.md`; route selection does not authorize production changes.

## Selected system: Claim Register

Three horizontal bands form an abstract N. The refined diagonal improves recognition while keeping the ledger structure. Thin horizontal rules, aligned financial records, generous space and deliberate typography carry the register idea into applications. Warmth comes from ivory and clear human language. Avoid making every surface look like a spreadsheet.

The earlier Resolved Path recommendation is superseded by the user's selection. Claim Register's small-size legibility remains a refinement concern; round 02 includes actual CSS-size specimens at 16, 24, 32, 48 and 64px. Use 24px as the provisional minimum.

## Mark and lockups

Proposed symbol construction uses a 64-unit square. Clear space: 16 units on each side, measured outside the mark's bounds. Keep this space free of text, edges and partner marks. Recommended digital minimum: 24px; 16px is a test specimen pending optical refinement, not an approved favicon. Provisional print minimum: 6mm, subject to a physical proof. Symbol and wordmark gap: half symbol width. Product name follows Nomine at a lower weight; do not construct independent product symbols.

Use onyx on ivory or ivory on onyx. Do not stretch, rotate, add shadows, recolor endpoints to imply statuses, or place over detailed imagery. The review wordmark is live editable type, not a finalized proprietary wordmark. Final delivery must include outlined masters plus an editable text source with font license provenance. Font substitution in a viewer is not typography approval.

## Colour and typography

Onyx #0B0B0D; warm ivory #F3F0E9; graphite #6B6B72; signal violet #665CF6. Round-02 proposed extensions: limestone #E5E1D8 for layered neutral surfaces, deep violet #5546D9 for accessible actions/small text on ivory, and pale violet #B7AEFF for a sparse dark-surface accent. These are neutral and tonal extensions, not independent accent colours. Proposed dark-surface secondary text #B9B6B0. Violet is for the main action or a specific available/verified state, always with a label. Completed and Expired can remain neutral with explicit text and distinct icons. Never use violet alone to establish verification.

Instrument Sans: headings 500, body 400, labels/buttons 600. IBM Plex Mono: amounts, addresses, transaction references and receipt metadata. Round 02 loads locally packaged fonts from `brand/assets/fonts/`, with OFL licenses alongside. Round 01 remains a historical fallback-type study. Use tabular figures for money. Preserve asset units and precision; no silent rounding of settlement amounts.

Proposed scale: display 80/84 desktop, 44/48 mobile; H1 48/52; H2 32/38; body 16/24; supporting labels 14/20. Financial terms are never microprint. Line length 45–75 characters. Sentence case. Avoid excessive tracking except short overlines.

## Layout, shape, imagery and data

12-column desktop / 4-column mobile grid; 24px desktop gutters, 16px mobile; max content width 1200px. Spacing scale 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 96. Default 24px panel padding. Thin structural rules; meaningful controls require adequate contrast. Primarily square fields with at most 4px control radius. No glass, glow, decorative gradients or floating finance cards.

Iconography: 24px grid, consistent 1.5–2px strokes; status icons always paired with words. Use simple lifecycle diagrams and typographic information. If photography is needed, show real people in ordinary contexts, monochrome, without inventing endorsements. Avoid stock crypto imagery, coins, chain links, envelopes, gift-box master marks and wallet hexagons.

Receipts: issuer, amount/asset, actual status, network, full timestamp and transaction reference. Public views exclude private eligibility and raw identity data. Truncate addresses visually only with access to the complete value; distinguish transaction hash from internal Claim reference. Always link to the actual transaction in working products; exploration examples are visibly labeled and contain no fake explorer links.

## Accessibility and motion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA: text 4.5:1, large text 3:1, meaningful control boundaries/focus indicators 3:1. Measure exact color pairs before implementation. Do not use graphite as small text on onyx, onyx small text on violet, or violet small text on ivory. White button text is preferred on violet; see generated contrast report. Keyboard focus needs a visible outline with offset; do not remove it for aesthetic reasons. Aim for 44px targets, support 200% zoom and narrow layouts, and use status text in addition to color. Logos need an accessible name when linked; decorative duplicates are hidden from assistive technology.

Motion: 160–220ms opacity/position feedback, no continuous pulsing or forced celebratory animation. An explanatory path may resolve once after a user action; it must not imply chain confirmation prematurely. Respect prefers-reduced-motion with immediate state changes. Keep pending and completed states distinct.
