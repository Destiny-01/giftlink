# Round 01 review notes

Rendered all three boards, application studies and mark specimens in the Codex browser. No visible clipping was observed at the review-panel width. The SVG boards scale to the panel, so their labeled pixel sizes describe source dimensions, not exact displayed screen pixels. Native-size optical validation remains part of finalization.

Contrast calculations: onyx/ivory 17.28:1; graphite/ivory 4.65:1; white/violet 4.74:1. Violet/ivory is only 4.17:1 and must not be used for normal-size text. Graphite/onyx and onyx/violet also fail normal-text AA. Use ivory or the proposed light secondary text on dark surfaces.

Visual judgment: Resolved Path remains legible in the scaled specimens and has the clearest N silhouette. Named Space has a stronger framing system but feels more technical. Claim Register's separated bands lose the N reading earlier and need greater optical development if selected.

These are first-round identity and composition studies. They are not finished product screens, font-validated wordmarks, registered marks, or production assets. Final avatar/banner sizing, outlined type, complete Claim/receipt templates and optical masters follow the user's route review.
