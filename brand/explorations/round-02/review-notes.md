# Claim Register — refinement 02

Route selected by the user. Final mark and palette approval pending. Production is untouched.

## Design recommendation

Build beauty through proportion, typography and restrained colour. Use a warm light inbox and an ink-led public presentation. Limestone provides surface depth without shadows or gradients. Use violet at decisive points rather than across entire panels. Do not import GiftLink's teal, gold or glow effects.

The original banded mark was difficult to read as an N. Round 02 keeps three separate register bands but introduces a consistent diagonal. Its black and ivory SVGs are editable vectors. The wordmark remains live Instrument Sans; final kerning and outlined exports are not yet complete.

Deep violet #5546D9 is a proposed functional extension of the approved signal violet, not a silent replacement. The contrast report documents intended text pairs. Pale violet is only a tonal dark-surface extension. Keep one accent family.
